# Examination Policy

Document ID: DOC-002
Version: 2026.1
Status: Approved

## Repeating an examination

Students may repeat an examination according to the university academic regulations. A
student may repeat a failed examination in the next official examination period.

## Eligibility

Examination eligibility depends on attendance, registration status and completion of the
required coursework. A student who is not eligible may not enter the examination hall.

## Results

Examination results are published by the examinations office. A student may request a
review of an examination result within ten working days of publication.
