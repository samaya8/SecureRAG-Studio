# Student Attendance Policy

Document ID: DOC-001
Version: 2026.1
Status: Approved

## Minimum attendance requirement

The minimum attendance requirement is 75%. Students must attend at least 75% of the
scheduled sessions of every registered course.

## Absence

Students who exceed the permitted absence limit may be prevented from completing the
course assessment unless an approved exception applies.

An absence is recorded when a student does not attend a scheduled session. A student may
request an excused absence by submitting supporting documents to the department within
five working days of the missed session.

## Consequences

A student whose attendance falls below the minimum attendance requirement receives a
written warning from the department. Continued absence after a written warning may lead
to exclusion from the final assessment of the course.
